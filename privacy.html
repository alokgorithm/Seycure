On-Device Privacy

Privacy Policy for Seycure

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

Developer: ArkQube (Alok Nath) · Contact: arkqube@gmail.com

Core Promise: Seycure is designed with privacy-first architecture. Your photos, screenshots, and documents are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded to any remote server or stored in the cloud.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Images and Screenshots: Redaction and optical character recognition (OCR) run 100% locally on your device using Android ML Kit. No visual data or text extracted from screenshots ever leaves your phone.
  • Files and Documents: Metadata scrubbing from PDF and DOCX documents happens locally. We do not transmit or retain your documents.
  • Personal Information: We do not ask for or collect your name, phone number, address, or email address. There are no user accounts or logins required.

2. Link Shield and URL Analysis

When you submit a URL or scan a QR code using Link Shield:

  • The submitted URL is transmitted over an encrypted HTTPS connection to our Cloudflare edge proxy (seycure-safe-browsing.arka-cmd.workers.dev) solely to resolve redirect chains and check threat reputation against the Google Safe Browsing API.
  • This URL lookup is processed ephemerally in memory and is never logged, stored in a database, or linked to your identity or device ID.

3. In-App Purchases & Payments

Seycure offers an optional one-time purchase ("Seycure Pro") to unlock advanced features. All transactions are processed securely and directly by Google Play Billing. ArkQube does not receive, process, or store your payment card or banking information.

4. Advertising

The free tier of Seycure offers optional Google AdMob Rewarded Video ads, which users may choose to watch to obtain extra daily auto-detection quota. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) in accordance with Google's privacy policy to serve ads and prevent fraudulent impressions. The ad SDK is disabled entirely for Pro users.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because Seycure does not maintain server-side accounts or databases of user files, there is no personal data retained. Custom redaction rules and daily usage quotas are stored locally on your device via Android private storage and can be cleared at any time by resetting learned rules inside Settings or uninstalling the app.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at arkqube@gmail.com.
